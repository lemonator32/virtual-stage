Virtual Stage is a concept project designed to bring the live concert experience into the home through virtual reality (VR) and web browsers. It acts as a platform to connect artists and fans globally, offering an alternative for those who cannot attend real-life events due to travel or scheduling constraints.

**Key Features & Mechanics:**

* **Real-Time Motion Capture:** Instead of a traditional video feed, performing artists send their live motion capture data directly to a server. The server then renders a full 3D environment and broadcasts it to viewers.
* **Interactive Audience Experience:** Viewers can actively enter the virtual crowd as customizable avatars. They can move around in real time, see other live fans, and interact with the crowd surrounding them.
* **Cross-Device Accessibility:** The experience supports both VR headsets and standard web browsers (using first-person keyboard/mouse controls).
* **Server-Side Rendering:** Because the visual environments are rendered completely on the host server rather than individual local devices, it remains accessible on lower-spec hardware while protecting proprietary 3D assets from being stolen.
